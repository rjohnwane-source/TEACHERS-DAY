Dear Mima Rose Ann,

Ma’am Rose Ann Miones thank you for being more than just a teacher, Almost 3 years na since natapos yung Senior High School but until now fresh pa rin yung memories ng section with you. The reminders, kwentuhan, tawanan, and even yung mga simple moments sa classroom. Hindi lang po kayo nagturo sa amin about sa subject, pero marami rin po kayong tinuro na lessons na hanggang ngayon dala pa rin namin. You always guided us, encouraged us, and reminded us to do our best. Looking back, I can say  na sobrang thankful kami na naging teacher namin kayo. Your patience, kindness, and dedication really made our SHS days more memorable. Thank you po for everything, Ma’am. Almost 3 years na pero parang kahapon lang yung mga memories with you. We may have moved on to different paths after SHS, pero you will always be one of the teachers na hindi namin makakalimutan. 💗

Thank you for everything. ❤️
